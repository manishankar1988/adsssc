About the Conference

Welcome to ADSSSC 2027

The Second International Conference on AI-Driven Solutions for Sustainable Smart Cities: Challenges and Opportunities (ADSSSC 2027) provides an international platform for academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and students to present and discuss innovative engineering and technology solutions for sustainable smart cities.

The conference highlights how artificial intelligence and multidisciplinary engineering can address urban challenges in mobility, energy, healthcare, public infrastructure, environmental monitoring, and resilience. It brings together experts from computing, electronics, electrical, civil, biomedical, and biotechnology domains, with an emphasis on responsible, practical, and scalable innovation aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

The programme will include keynote lectures, technical paper sessions, tutorials, panel discussions, special sessions, and structured networking.

Call for Papers

Conference Tracks

Original, unpublished contributions are invited on advances in artificial intelligence, data analytics, smart systems, and emerging technologies that support sustainable urban development. Interdisciplinary submissions are encouraged, subject to the applicable IEEE scope and publication requirements.

Track 1

Artificial Intelligence and Data-Driven Smart Systems

  • AI for smart-city infrastructure, intelligent transportation, and urban mobility
  • Machine learning and deep learning for real-time analytics and decision support
  • AI for resource management, energy optimization, and disaster resilience
Track 2

Smart and Renewable Energy Systems

  • AI-enabled smart grids and energy-efficient power systems
  • AI and IoT for renewable-energy optimization
  • Intelligent energy storage, forecasting, and management
Track 3

Electronics, IoT, Edge Intelligence, and Automation

  • IoT, sensors, embedded systems, and urban automation
  • Industrial automation and smart manufacturing
  • Edge computing, cyber-physical systems, and real-time processing
Track 4

Biomedical and Healthcare Innovations

  • AI in medical imaging, wearable technology, and remote monitoring
  • IoT-enabled healthcare and connected health systems
  • Healthcare analytics and intelligent decision support
Track 5

Interdisciplinary Engineering for Sustainability

  • Environmental monitoring and climate-resilient infrastructure
  • Secure data architectures, privacy, and trustworthy AI
  • Sustainable materials, smart buildings, and urban infrastructure

For Authors

Paper Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must be original and not previously published or under review elsewhere.
  • Manuscripts must follow the IEEE conference template.
  • Maximum paper length: 6 pages, including references, subject to the applicable IEEE policy.
  • Submissions undergo double-blind peer review; remove all author names, affiliations, and identifying information.
  • The online submission link will be published with the Call for Papers on 15 January 2027.

Publication

*Publication in IEEE Xplore® is intended, subject to formal IEEE approval and compliance with IEEE requirements. Copyright, licensing, and indexing arrangements will follow the final agreement and applicable IEEE policies.

Awards

Best Paper and Young Researcher awards will recognize outstanding contributions, subject to committee approval.

Review Process

  1. Submissions are assigned an ID and mapped to the appropriate track.
  2. Initial screening for scope, formatting, similarity, and basic quality.
  3. Double-blind technical review by the Technical Program Committee.
  4. Authors revise their papers to address reviewer comments.
  5. Revised papers are re-evaluated for compliance.
  6. Final decision based on TPC recommendations.
  7. Camera-ready papers are checked and confirmed.
  8. Registration is verified for inclusion in the programme and proceedings.

Research Ethics and AI Usage

All submissions are checked for similarity at initial screening and before final acceptance. Conflict-of-interest and research-ethics procedures are documented by the TPC.

AI-based tools may be used for language editing only. AI-generated ideas, analyses, or results without human oversight are not permitted, and any AI use must be disclosed in the manuscript in line with the IEEE Publication Ethics and Integrity Policy.

Venue

Sahrdaya College of Engineering and Technology

Sahrdaya College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), established by the Irinjalakuda Diocesan Educational Trust, is affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University and approved by AICTE. It offers engineering programmes in Biotechnology, Biomedical, Civil, Computer Science, Electrical and Electronics, and Electronics and Communication Engineering, along with postgraduate and doctoral research programmes.

The campus offers a multimedia conference hall, five seminar halls, and a multipurpose indoor stadium, and is home to an active IEEE Student Branch and the Gram Vista CPS Smart Village Living Lab.

Address: Kodakara, Thrissur District, Kerala 680684, India
View on Google Maps

Thrissur is renowned for its festivals, historic temples, and cultural heritage, including the famous Thrissur Pooram. Nearby attractions include Vadakkumnathan Temple, Shakthan Thampuran Palace, Punnathur Kotta, and the Athirappilly Waterfalls. The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK).
