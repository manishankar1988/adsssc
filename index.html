About the Conference

The Second International Conference on AI-Driven Solutions for Sustainable Smart Cities: Challenges and Opportunities (ADSSSC 2027) provides an international platform for academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and students to present and discuss innovative engineering and technology solutions for sustainable smart cities.

The conference highlights how artificial intelligence and multidisciplinary engineering can address urban challenges in mobility, energy, healthcare, public infrastructure, environmental monitoring, and resilience. It brings together experts from computing, electronics, electrical, civil, biomedical, and biotechnology domains, with an emphasis on responsible, practical, and scalable innovation aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

ADSSSC 2027 will feature keynote lectures, technical paper sessions, tutorials, panel discussions, special sessions, and structured networking, fostering collaboration between academia, industry, start-ups, and policymakers.

Building on the First Edition

The first edition, held on 9–10 April 2026, received 893 paper submissions, accepted 301 papers (an acceptance rate of approximately 33.7%), and recorded 147 registered participants. The second edition builds on this foundation with stronger international outreach, track-level review coordination, and industry engagement. View the ADSSSC 2026 archive.

About Sahrdaya

Sahrdaya College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), established by the Irinjalakuda Diocesan Educational Trust, is affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University and approved by AICTE.

The college offers engineering programmes in Biotechnology, Biomedical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Electronics and Communication Engineering, along with postgraduate programmes and doctoral research in relevant disciplines.

Sahrdaya hosts established innovation and research facilities that support interdisciplinary work, an active IEEE Student Branch, and has organised events including the IEEE International Conference ADSSSC 2026, IEEE Infinia, Google AI Campus Fest, and IEEE–Altair events.

Author Guidelines

Technical papers describing original, previously unpublished research are invited in all conference tracks. Authors must use the official IEEE conference templates for preparing manuscripts: IEEE Manuscript Templates .

Full papers may be up to 6 pages, subject to the applicable IEEE conference template and policy. The paper submission link will be announced with the Call for Papers on 15 January 2027. All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review based on originality, technical quality, relevance, and clarity; authors must remove all identifying information from submitted manuscripts.

Paper Selection Process

  1. Step 1 — Received: Submitted papers are assigned an ID and mapped to the appropriate track.
  2. Step 2 — Screening: Initial checks for scope, formatting, similarity, and basic quality.
  3. Step 3 — Review: Eligible papers are sent for double-blind technical review.
  4. Step 4 — Revision: Authors submit revised versions addressing reviewer comments.
  5. Step 5 — Second Review: Revised papers are re-evaluated for compliance with recommendations.
  6. Step 6 — Decision: Final accept / reject decision is taken based on TPC recommendations.
  7. Step 7 — Camera-Ready: Camera-ready papers are checked and confirmed.
  8. Step 8 — Registration: Registration status is verified for inclusion in the program and proceedings.

Publication in IEEE Xplore® is intended, subject to formal IEEE approval and compliance with IEEE requirements. Copyright, licensing, and indexing arrangements will follow the final agreement and applicable IEEE policies.

For queries related to submissions or reviews, contact the Technical Program Committee at adsssc@sahrdaya.ac.in.

Awards

Outstanding contributions will be recognized through Best Paper and Young Researcher awards, subject to committee approval.

Plagiarism and AI Usage Policy

All submissions will be checked for similarity using a recognized similarity-checking process, at initial screening and before final acceptance. Authors are responsible for ensuring that similarity indices comply with IEEE international conference norms. Review, conflict-of-interest, and research-ethics procedures will be documented.

Authors may use AI-based tools for language editing and grammar checks; however, AI-generated content that introduces ideas, analyses, or results without human oversight is not permitted. Any use of AI tools must be transparently disclosed within the manuscript, in line with IEEE Publication Ethics and Integrity Policy.

About Thrissur

Thrissur, known as the “Cultural Capital of Kerala,” is renowned for its vibrant festivals, historic temples, and rich cultural heritage. The city hosts the famous Thrissur Pooram, one of the most spectacular temple festivals in India.

Attractions in and around Thrissur include:

  • Vadakkumnathan Temple: An ancient Lord Shiva temple with classic Kerala architecture and murals.
  • Thrissur Zoo and State Museum: Exhibits regional wildlife, history, and culture.
  • Punnathur Kotta Elephant Sanctuary: A unique elephant sanctuary offering close-up views of trained elephants.
  • Shakthan Thampuran Palace: A restored palace museum highlighting Thrissur’s royal legacy.
  • Athirappilly Waterfalls: Often called the “Niagara of India,” located a short drive from the city.

With its welcoming atmosphere and diverse attractions, Thrissur provides an enriching cultural backdrop for conference participants.
